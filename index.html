jejemon sub-culture

year: 2010

context :

Originally a style of shorthand typing through the short messaging service, in which each text message sent by a cellphone is limited to 160 characters, it became a popular cultural phenomenon in the Philippines, extending to fashion and music characterized by a bold style influenced by hip-hop.

A youth trend around 2010 that influenced Filipino self-image through colorful caps, loose clothes, hip-hop fashion, accessories, poses, and unique texting styles. Young Filipinos became interested because it was fun, creative, popular among friends, and easy to spread through mobile phones and social media. However, Jejemons were also stereotyped and judged because of their appearance, language, and perceived social class.
